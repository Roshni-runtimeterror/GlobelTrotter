## ✈️ About GlobeTrotter

**GlobeTrotter** is a modern travel planning platform designed to make trip planning simple, personalized and interactive.

Plan multi-city journeys, discover destinations and activities, manage your itinerary, track your budget and share your travel plans with others.

> **Your Journey. Your Interests. Your Plan.**

---

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🔐 **Login & Register** | Secure user authentication |
| ✈️ **Create Trip** | Create personalized multi-city trips |
| 📍 **Nearby Locations** | Discover nearby and recommended places |
| 🏖️ **Travel Interests** | Choose interests like beaches, hiking, food & nature |
| ✨ **AI Travel Guide** | Get recommendations based on your interests |
| 🗺️ **Itinerary Builder** | Organize cities, activities, dates & times |
| 💰 **Budget Planner** | Track estimated travel expenses in ₹ INR |
| 📅 **Calendar View** | Visualize your complete trip schedule |
| 🔎 **Activity Search** | Find activities based on category, cost & duration |
| 👥 **Community** | Explore and share travel plans |
| 🔗 **Trip Sharing** | Share a read-only version of your itinerary |
| 👤 **Profile** | Manage personal information and preferences |
